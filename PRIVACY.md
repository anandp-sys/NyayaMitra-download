# Nyaya Mitra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Nyaya Mitra ("the app") is a free legal-information app built by Anand. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, no login, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The legal guides and law library are stored inside the app and work offline. What you type or say into the search box stays on your phone.

## Voice features

- **Speak (voice typing)** uses your phone's own speech-recognition service (usually Google). That service converts your speech to text under its own privacy policy. The app only receives the resulting text.
- **Listen (read aloud)** uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine and works on the phone.

## Optional AI answers

If you choose to add your own Google Gemini API key in Settings and tap **Ask AI**, the question you typed is sent directly from your phone to Google's Gemini API to generate an answer. It is never sent to the developer. Google handles that data under the [Gemini API Additional Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). On Google's free tier, Google may use the content to improve its products. Do not include names, phone numbers, ID numbers or other personal details in AI questions.

Your API key is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage, and is used only to call the Gemini API.

## Links and calls

Tapping a website link opens your browser. Tapping **Call** opens your phone's dialer with the number filled in; nothing is dialed automatically.

## Children

The app is a general-audience information tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Not legal advice

The app provides general legal information, not legal advice, and is not affiliated with any government body.
