# Nyaya Mitra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Nyaya Mitra ("the app") is a free legal-information app created by Anand Priy. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, no login, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The legal guides and law library are stored inside the app and work offline. What you type or say into the search box stays on your phone.

## Voice features

- **Speak (voice typing)** uses your phone's own speech-recognition service (usually Google). That service converts your speech to text under its own privacy policy. The app only receives the resulting text.
- **Listen (read aloud)** uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine and works on the phone.

## Ask Anand Priy (only when you tap it)

The offline guides never use the internet. "Ask Anand Priy" gives automatic answers generated by Google's Gemini AI, and is used only when you tap it:

- **Free (default):** your question, together with the text of the matching built-in guides, is sent over an encrypted connection to Nyaya Mitra's small server (run on Cloudflare Workers), which passes it to Google's Gemini API and returns the answer. The server does not save your questions or answers. To stop misuse, the app also sends a random ID created on your phone; it is used only to limit how many questions one phone can ask per minute and is not linked to your name, number or account.
- **Your own key (optional):** if you add your own Google Gemini API key in Settings, your question goes directly from your phone to Google's Gemini API and does not pass through Nyaya Mitra's server. The key is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage.

In both cases Google handles the question under the [Gemini API Additional Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). On Google's free tier, Google may use the content to improve its products. Do not include names, phone numbers, ID numbers or other personal details in your questions.

## Links and calls

Tapping a website link opens your browser. Tapping **Call** opens your phone's dialer with the number filled in; nothing is dialed automatically.

## Children

The app is a general-audience information tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Not legal advice

The app provides general legal information, not legal advice, and is not affiliated with any government body.
